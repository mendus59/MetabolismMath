For learning, not medical advice. Library entries summarize published human data, mainly the NIH Office of Dietary Supplements fact sheets. Intake references are US adult values. The formula-only prediction uses simple rules (polarity, size, elements, the features you tick). It's meant to show how chemists reason about this, and it will be wrong for some molecules. Erythritol is a good example: it looks like a sugar but passes through unchanged. Energy uses Thornton's rule (~440 kJ per mole of O₂ consumed), with nitrogen assumed to leave as urea. Structures are parsed in your browser by RDKit: it supplies Crippen logP, polar surface area, the full Rule-of-5 checks and automatic detection of functional groups.
